# Data creation

This experiment rebuilds portable three-class classification and RAG-tuning datasets from scratch. Human experts choose the legal articles, assess the collected documents, supply rules for making controlled variants, and set the release criteria. Scripts perform deterministic splitting, extraction, retrieval, validation, and export; subagents apply expert-approved modification rules and propose semantic labels. The corpus contains **original Russian court rulings**. Appeal decisions remain a separate evidence pool: an appeal need not be matched to every corpus document and does not by itself prove a contradiction.

The 1,000 reviewed rulings are frozen by document or related case into independent `train`, `val`, and `test` sets of 600/200/200 **before** sentence processing. Every derived variant remains in its source split. Sentences come from the final `ПОСТАНОВИЛ` section using paragraph-aware `razdel` splitting and an audited irrelevant-text filter. Original files remain untouched; minimal subagent-proposed edits are applied to separate text copies, after which hypotheses, premises, and context are rebuilt.

For every hypothesis, resolve explicit КоАП citations before filling the candidate set with semantic retrieval from the October 2026 lawbook. Collapse duplicate legal units while preserving every retrieval route. Each pair receives one NLI label: `not mentioned`, `entailment`, or `contradiction`, plus an independent `related`/`unrelated` relevance judgment for RAG. Keep the full labeled pool and publish weights: train NLI sampling targets 50% contradiction, 25% entailment, and 25% not mentioned; RAG sampling targets 50% related and 50% unrelated. Validation targets about 120 contradiction-bearing documents and test about 40. These evaluation distributions are deliberately enriched and do not estimate deployment prevalence.

Experts review every synthetic variant and every `val` and `test` pair. They review a stratified sample of other `train` pairs across articles, labels, and rules, plus every uncertain pair. Each split is a movable folder containing its CSV and the exact original or edited UTF-8 decision texts referenced by it. Permanent `row_id` values survive row deletion and reordering. Released records preserve source, variant and edit provenance, citations, retrieval routes, proposed and reviewed judgments, and review status. Keep private inputs and outputs under ignored `local/` paths; do not overwrite the historical `val.csv` or `Full` benchmark.

The expert-selected articles for this collection are 18.8, 18.9, 18.10, 18.11, 18.12, 18.15, 18.16, 18.17, 18.18, 18.19, 18.20, and 19.27 of the КоАП РФ. Articles 20.20, 20.21, and 6.9 form a separate domain-shift test candidate pool. Original rulings and decisions on complaints are collected independently under ignored `local/data/classification/dataset0929/`. Collection targets are at least 1,000 distinct original rulings for the first group, about 30 for the domain-shift group, and 5–10 appeal decisions per article where available. These are candidate documents pending expert review; collection does not assign pair labels or split the original rulings.

The expert exclusion review is implemented in `review_decisions.py`. It checks the main original-ruling pool for independent federal-law references in the final operative section, entrepreneurs as the accused party, sentence separation of the article/fine/imposed expulsion or an uncited departure obligation, damaged spacing, and combined offences or episodes. References to a federal law embedded in a cited КоАП provision are retained; mentioning an entrepreneur as an employer or in a statutory quotation does not exclude a ruling. A separate article and fine remain eligible when expulsion is not imposed. Ambiguous spacing and multi-episode candidates require a documented local review decision before applying the plan. Reports retain passages, provenance and hashes; excluded DOCX/HTML/TXT bundles are archived outside the active decisions tree and the original manifest is preserved. This review does not assign NLI labels or dataset splits.

The implemented dataset workflow and design are described in [Series 01](series_01_nli_rag/README.md). Collection and local commands are in the [data-creation instructions](instructions.md).

`filter_decisions_by_date.py` applies a configurable date cutoff to main original rulings. It checks the published opening before archive title metadata, records the date passages, and archives older source bundles with their provenance. Unresolved dates require review. The date operation uses the same hash checks and rollback as the expert exclusion review; domain-shift originals and appeals remain separate.
