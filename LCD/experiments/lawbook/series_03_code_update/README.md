# Series 03: update the premise-search lawbook

The current corpus workflow builds a new local premise-search database from **only the supplied КоАП РФ DOCX**, labelled edition 01.10.2026. Federal laws and historical `dms-rag` texts are not inputs. The original federal-law inventory/coverage audit described below remains a separate historical preparation step.

`LCD.shared.coderag` extracts the smallest legal units independently within each article branch: пункты where present, otherwise части, otherwise the article. Shared introductions and general list closing rules accompany their points; point-specific continuations stay with that point. Sanctions and nested подпункты stay intact. DOCX styles separate ГАРАНТ commentary and amendment metadata from operative text. Repealed/non-applicable placeholders, front matter and signatures are excluded. Dotted, hyphenated and superscript-style identifiers retain their legal identity.

The supplied-document export has **4,065 legal units** and **270 individual notes**, covering 1,159 encountered article headings (including inactive headings). `koap_units.csv` has `text,source,hierarchy,changes`; `koap_notes.csv` has unmodified note text and its article/chapter/section position. Notes are not indexed. The paragraph audit accounts for retained and excluded source material; source/output hashes and the filename edition label are recorded. The label is not an independent determination of legal applicability on a particular date.

The new index uses Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B and normalized 1,024-dimensional vectors in an exact cosine FAISS `IndexFlatIP`. **One CSV row remains one vector**: no character chunks, overlap or silent tokenizer truncation. Actual token lengths are checked before embedding. Query inputs receive this instruction, stored in the index manifest:

> Given a statement from a Russian court decision, retrieve provisions of Russian law that regulate the same legal issue, including rules that may support or contradict the statement, while prioritizing matching actors, actions, conditions, and procedural stages.

Documents receive no instruction. The corpus, notes, FAISS index, readable JSONL metadata and audit artifacts live under ignored `local/data/coderag/`; model downloads use ignored `local/cache/huggingface/`. The JSON metadata preserves structural IDs, complete text, normalized citations, titles, hierarchy, amendment information and source/edition/paragraph provenance. Loading validates artifact hashes, dimensions and row counts. Structured exact lookup works without loading the encoder; semantic search lazily loads the matching model and stored query instruction.

FAISS is appropriate for this fixed local corpus and preserves a simple file-based workflow. Its [flat index performs exhaustive search](https://faiss.ai/cpp_api/struct/structfaiss_1_1IndexFlat.html). [Qdrant's integrated hybrid queries](https://qdrant.tech/documentation/search/hybrid-queries/) become useful if a future workflow needs database-managed sparse/dense fusion or extensive metadata filtering. Lexical/regex candidate generation and reranking can be added independently using the current structural IDs. Neither is implemented here; existing pipeline defaults and historical indexes are preserved. A retrieval-quality evaluation is separate from index-construction smoke checks.

Before building the replacement database, audit the full text of `local/data/classification/dataset0929/decisions` (both main and domain-shift groups, excluding appeals) for federal-law references. Give the expert one representative sentence per identifiable law, retaining its source decision and preferring the first dated citation. Distinguish different laws sharing the same number using citation dates and titles; matching descriptive titles merge date variants for the same number. Keep every original matching sentence in a companion full export. Compare explicit law numbers with the supplied inventory to flag potential omissions; the expert must resolve references without numbers and confirm titles, dates, and relevant editions.

The local audit is implemented. Expert review and additional-law coverage remain pending. The new КоАП-only database is built separately from this audit; integration with the full contradiction-detection pipeline remains future work. Explicit citations must retain priority over semantic retrieval, and premise reports must retain source and version provenance. See the [lawbook instructions](../instructions.md) for corpus commands and the earlier audit workflow.

The initial audit on 2026-10-06 scanned all 1,068 decision DOCX files and exported 5,260 matching sentence occurrences from 1,034 decisions. It flagged 34 explicit law numbers absent from the numbered-law filenames for expert review; 2,091 sentences had no explicit numbered ФЗ identifier. These are provisional coverage candidates: number-only matching does not resolve the КоАП title, distinguish laws with the same number in different years, or decide which amending laws belong in the future database.

The compact expert export now retains 41 representative sentences covering 44 provisional law identities; sentences citing several laws are printed once. The original 5,260 occurrences remain available in `federal_law_sentences_full.txt`.
